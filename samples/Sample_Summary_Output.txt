This educational demonstration explores an extractive text summarizer for research papers. [p. 1]

The system retrieves salient sentences from the document instead of generating new factual claims. [p. 1]

Each selected sentence retains a page reference when a PDF is supplied. [p. 1]

Automated summarization can provide a first-pass reading aid, but selected sentences should always be checked against the original source. [p. 1]

Earlier graph-based methods model sentences as connected nodes and use their similarity to estimate importance. [p. 1]

Selected sentences are sorted back into their original document order for readability. [p. 1]

This educational prototype combines PDF parsing, TF-IDF features, sentence similarity, graph centrality, and redundancy-aware selection. [p. 2]

Its main advantage is traceability because summary sentences can be checked in the original PDF. [p. 2]
